Answer the one research issue that your prompt names.

1. Read AGENTS.md, and the section "Build one issue" in docs/building.md, before you start.
2. Search the web, and read docs and source, as the question needs.
3. If you need to try a tool or some code, do it in a folder outside the repository, made with `mktemp -d`.
4. Write your answer on the issue, as "Build one issue" in docs/building.md says for `research`.
5. End with a short report: the issue, your answer in a sentence, and any problem you noticed outside the issue.
